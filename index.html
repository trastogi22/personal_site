I seek to understand our lived experience and shape the world for the better.

For years, that meant expression through artwork and poetry. Lately it means climate tech, markets, and policy.

Stanford, CA  ·  trastogi@stanford.edu  ·  LinkedIn  ·  ArtStation

Photo

I started drawing early in life, mostly portraits and family scenes: beautiful textiles, a winter afternoon in Delhi, a street in SF Chinatown. This requires observation, hours of analyzing every shape and color present in an image.

I didn't plan on using that skill for research, but it's turned out useful. When I was modeling carbon accounting frameworks, or trying to figure out a state's legislation on tutoring policy, the process was surprisingly similar: sit with something for a while until I'm able to replicate and improve upon its patterns.

Markets are a system built from people. I became interested in investing because I want to act on what patterns reveal about people. What's working and what's not? What needs more attention?
